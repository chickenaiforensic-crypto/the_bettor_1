## How a gap closes

1. Item arrives → dropped into the workspace (pack → `data/packs/`, legacy app → `reference/`, export → `data/seeds/`).
2. A `ZONES.md` entry records arrival + md5 + what changes.
3. The affected gate re-runs and the difference vs the gap-fallback is documented line by line.
