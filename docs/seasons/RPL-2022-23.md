## Anomaly A1 — the awarded game (kept verbatim as played)

`19/03/2023 Pari NN 1–1 Torpedo Moscow` — played 1–1, but Pari NN fielded the
ineligible Yaroslav Mikhaylov (untracked caution ban carried from lower-league
games). The RFU awarded Torpedo a **3–0 win** on 22/03/2023. Consequences:

* Official table rows: Torpedo 13 pts (W3 D4 L23, 22/61); Pari NN 30 pts (8-6-16, 33/50).
* This dataset's rows: Torpedo 11 pts (2-5-23, 20/62); Pari NN 31 pts (8-7-15, 34/48).
* **Positions unchanged** (13th/16th); all other 14 rows match official tables exactly
  ([tribuna](https://tribuna.com/en/league/rpl/table/2022-2023/)), and the dataset's
  goal total (729) is exactly one short of the official 730 — the awarded game's
  accounting. Evidence: [Wikipedia 2022–23 RPL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Russian_Premier_League).
