## Anomaly A3 — playoffs NOT in the source file

The source jumps from the final round (24/05/2025) to the 2025/26 opener
(18/07/2025); the two playoff ties are absent — a source coverage gap, not deleted
data. Outcomes (from official reporting, documented here but **not** added to any
CSV, per [Wikipedia 2024–25 RPL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Russian_Premier_League)):

* **Akhmat 3–2 Ural on aggregate** — Akhmat stayed up. (Khimki failing licencing
  altered the pairings: FNL 4th Ural played Akhmat, FNL 5th Sochi played Pari NN.)
* **Sochi beat Pari NN** — Sochi promoted; Pari NN were then **reprieved** because
  Khimki (12th) were refused a 2025/26 RPL licence (24/05/2025) and later dissolved.
* **Orenburg**, initially relegated as 15th, were readmitted on **11/07/2025** after
  First-League champions **Torpedo Moscow** were excluded for attempted
  match-fixing committed in 2024/25.
* Net 2025/26 intake confirmed by next season's roster in this dataset: **Baltika**
  (First League champions) and **Sochi** up; **Fakel** and **Khimki** out.
